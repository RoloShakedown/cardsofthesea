Ship's log

    How to play

    Objective

    Be the last player with an active Main Island card or ship. Knock rivals out by destroying all of their island cards and ships.

    Setup
    • Each player takes a suit. Their 2, 8 and Q of that suit start face-up as their Main Island.
    • Two Jokers are shuffled into the deck. Everyone is dealt 7 cards.
    • Aces are low (1). J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13. A card's strength is its value.
    Your turn

    Draw 1 card, then choose one action: Build, Attack, Cycle or Pass.

    Build
    • Fortify: put a card of your island's suit on your Main Island.
    • Launch a ship: play a set of 3 to 6 cards of one suit that isn't your island's suit. The ship takes that suit. Each card in it is one hit it can take.
    • Reinforce a ship: add 1 or more cards of a ship's suit to that ship, including one that's been damaged. A ship holds at most 6 cards. The launch bonus doesn't trigger again.
    • Island lost: once your Main Island is destroyed, you can't rebuild it, launch new ships or reinforce the ships you have. You keep your surviving ships and can still attack, cycle or pass.
    • When a ship launches, pick one bonus. Destroyer: 1 free attack. Supply Boat: draw 2 extra cards. Radar Ship: look at and reorder the top N cards of the draw pile, where N is the number of players.
    Attack
    • Same-Suit Strike: discard a higher card of the island card's suit.
    • Matching-Value Strike: discard a card of the same value in another suit to destroy an island card.
    • Off-Suit Volley: discard cards not of the target's suit, all at once. Each deals 1 damage, and you need damage equal to the island card's value.
    • Attacking ships: each card of the ship's suit knocks 1 card off the ship, whatever its value. Every 2 off-suit cards knock off 1, and they can be any mix of other suits. The attacker chooses which cards come off, and they go to the discard pile. Damage stays, so you can wear a ship down over several turns. It sinks when its last card is gone.
    • Several same-suit cards can attack together, adding their values. A 10♠ and 3♠ together destroy a K♠.
    • No mixing: one attack uses only cards of the target's suit, or only off-suit cards, never both.
    • Joker: destroy any card or ship. Or place it on your Main Island, where it acts as a King of your suit. It can be destroyed by any King, cards of your suit adding up to 13 or more, a 13-card off-suit volley, or the other Joker.
    Cycle and Pass
    • Cycle: after drawing, discard exactly 3 cards and draw 3 new cards from the top of the deck. This uses your action for the turn.
    • Pass: do nothing.
    Discards and winning

    Destroyed cards and used attack cards go to the discard pile. When the draw pile runs out, the discard pile is shuffled into a new deck. The last player with an island card or ship wins.
